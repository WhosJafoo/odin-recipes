Holiday Punch

Include this drink on a good sunny day

Stay hidrated with this delicious juice, you only need these ingredients!

  • ⅓ cup white sugar
  • 1 quart cranberry juice
  • 2 cups unsweetened pineapple juice
  • 3 tablespoons almond extract
  • 1 (2 liter) bottle ginger ale
  1. Combine sugar, cranberry juice, pineapple juice, and almond extract in a large container, mix together and refrigerate until chilled.
  2. To serve, pour juice mixture into a punch bowl. Stir in ginger ale.
